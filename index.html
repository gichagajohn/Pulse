Methodology

A disciplined process, not a black box

Each published selection passes through the same data, quality, and risk review.

Data collection

Fixtures and bookmaker prices are refreshed each matchday. Injuries and weather are included only when available from our providers; post-match results are never substituted for pre-match predictions.

Quality filtering

Matches with incomplete or unreliable data are removed before analysis. If nothing meets the bar, we publish “No Bet” rather than guess.

Risk-checked selection

Candidate picks pass through a separate risk and portfolio review designed to filter overconfident or inconsistent calls.

Every published outcome appears on the performance page—wins and losses alike. Read the full methodology and measurement guide.

About Football Pulse

Open methods. Inspectable records. Clear limits.

What the service does, how selections are produced, and how the public record should be interpreted.

A football analysis service—not a bookmaker

Football Pulse publishes probability-led football analysis and time-limited premium access to current selections. It does not accept bets, hold betting stakes, or promise returns. The service is informational and restricted to adults aged 18 and over.

Data and predictive core

The predictive core uses a Dixon–Coles-adjusted Poisson framework, supported by market baselines, price checks, calibration safeguards, and a separate selection-risk review. Fixtures, team statistics, bookmaker prices, and settled results come from the platform’s integrated data providers.

  • Only fixtures with sufficient usable data and executable recorded prices qualify.
  • Availability varies by competition and provider coverage; missing information is not invented.
  • Injuries and weather are considered only when available. Neither is claimed to guarantee better predictions.
  • “No Bet” remains a valid outcome when no selection improves risk-adjusted utility.

Probability, confidence, and price are different

Model confidence is a rating, not a guarantee and not automatically a calibrated probability for an individual market. Price-implied probability is calculated as 1 ÷ decimal odds and includes bookmaker margin. Football Pulse does not display an expected-value edge unless the required probability and executable-price inputs are genuinely available.

How performance is measured

The public dashboard uses settled published selections and retains wins, losses, and voids. Economic metrics use one-unit flat-stake singles at the recorded odds: wins profit odds minus one, losses lose one unit, and voids return stake. ROI and yield are equivalent under this convention.

These figures describe historical model output, not a customer’s actual betting returns. Monthly and league splits can contain small samples and should not be treated as proof of a lasting advantage.
