Methodology

A disciplined process, not a black box

Each published selection passes through the same data, quality, and risk review.

Data collection

Fixtures, odds, injuries, and weather are pulled fresh each matchday from live football data providers, never estimated after the fact.

Quality filtering

Matches with incomplete or unreliable data are removed before analysis. If nothing meets the bar, we publish “No Bet” rather than guess.

Risk-checked selection

Candidate picks pass through a separate risk and portfolio review designed to filter overconfident or inconsistent calls.
